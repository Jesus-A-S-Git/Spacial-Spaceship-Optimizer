What do these words mean?
  • Build: the main weapon type a ship relies on (lasers, missiles, hangars = fighters, cannons, boarding = teleports). A hybrid mixes two.
  • Anti-air / engines (anti-missile): rooms that shoot down incoming missiles and fighters.
  • Anti-teleport: rooms that stop enemy boarding teleports (security gates, repulsors, interdictors).
  • Lifts and Service vents: how crew move between rooms. Lifts go up and down, vents go sideways.
  • Power made vs needed: what the reactors produce compared with what all rooms would draw at full power.
  • Layout letters (GRA, CAR, REA...): initials of the room name. Hover a room in a layout for its full name.

Power: made and needed

For every top ship: the power its reactors make, and the power all its systems (weapons, shields, engines, crew rooms ...) draw when running at full power. The ladder data has no room levels, so every room is counted at the best level the ship's level allows. Players power systems as needed, so ships make far less than all their systems could draw. The room recommender uses each build's ratio.

Which rooms face the hull edge

How much of each room type's outline is hull edge, compared with the average room (log2 ratio: +1 = twice as often, -1 = half as often). The optimizer uses this as TAG_EDGE.

Which rooms sit next to each other

Touching room pairs compared with chance (log2 of observed / expected). Blue = top ships put these types together more often than chance, orange = they keep them apart. Pairs seen too rarely are left grey and are not used by the optimizer (TAG_ADJ). Hover a cell for the counts.
