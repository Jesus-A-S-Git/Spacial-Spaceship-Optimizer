    Weapon matchups

    Each weapon type has a counter: engines stop missiles, anti-air stops hangar craft, interdictor gates and traps stop boarders, and shields soak lasers and cannons. A counter can only stop so much per volley, so a few missiles into many engines are almost all stopped, while a big missile load swamps a few engines.

    Static numbers come from the rooms each ship has; "avg" numbers are averages over the simulated fights. Green marks the better side.

    How the estimate works
    • Every room is taken at the best level a level-14 ship can build (the layouts do not say which level each room has).
    • Each weapon fires a volley about every 10 seconds (with random timing). Its damage grows with the power the room draws, times the power the ship makes compared with what its rooms need (full output at the top-100 median, as top ships never power everything), plus 5% per booster room.
    • Lasers, cannons and missiles hit shields first; fighters and boarding do not. Engines shoot down missiles, anti-air shoots down fighters, and teleport gates stop boarding, more often the more of them a ship has. Cloak makes some shots miss, radar cancels it.
    • Shots hit a random room. Armor next to that room reduces the damage by the armor's level (Lv16: 22%) times how much of the room's outline touches armor. Rooms that take too much damage stop working (no repairs: no crew), so losing reactors costs power and losing shield rooms costs shields.
    • Both ships have the same hull HP (hull HP is not in the data). A fight ends when a hull reaches zero, or after 3 minutes (more hull left wins).
    • Each fight scales each side's damage by a random ±25% for everything not modelled. 2000 fights give the percentage.
    • Checks: a ship against itself comes out at about 50% (48-53% for single ships). Across all 4950 pairs of top-100 ships the better-ranked ship wins only 48% of the time: the top 100 run nearly the same rooms, so what separates them (crew, research, play) is exactly what this model leaves out.
    Data: top 100 of the PixyShip ladder and the official room list, refreshed every two weeks by the repository's GitHub Action.
